> About me

Who I am, what I am studying, and where I am headed.

I am Gio Duldoco, a third-year Information Technology student at Xavier University Ateneo de Cagayan. My focus is mobile application development and system architecture, and most of my coursework right now revolves around building responsive, cross-platform applications that behave well on real devices rather than only in a browser window.

I am still early in my degree, so my priority is building a solid foundation I can apply to real problems. My technical interests span hybrid mobile development with Apache Cordova, front-end architecture, event-driven programming and system hardening. I enjoy working through logical problems and shaping interfaces that are fast, readable and consistent from the smallest phone to a full desktop screen.
